Wire tournament stage awareness into the preflop and postflop decision methods, making HawkEyeBot adjust strategy for bubble, ITM, heads-up, and field exploitation.

Purpose: This is the final integration that makes the bot tournament-aware. The bot will tighten at bubble to survive into the money, release pressure when ITM to fight for 1st, widen dramatically in heads-up, and exploit the weak test field with thinner value bets. These are the strategic edges that differentiate a tournament-winning bot from one that plays fixed strategy.

Output: Modified _preflop, _facing_bet, and _bet_or_check methods with tournament stage conditionals, plus comprehensive integration tests proving each adjustment fires at the correct stage and does NOT fire at other stages.
